Analyze a transcript

Paste a support conversation in English, Hindi or Hinglish, one turn per line. Phone numbers, emails, UPI IDs and similar details are masked before the text is sent to the model.

Results appear here: the masked conversation, extracted labels, summary, next action and the agent checklist.

Conversations

Search stored conversations and open one to see its full analysis.

Select a conversation to see its details.

Batch processing

Process many conversations in the background. Conversations already processed by the model currently selected are skipped unless you choose to reprocess them; switching to a different model processes them again.

Upload a CSV

Required columns: conversation_id and transcript. Optional: language. Maximum 10 MB.

Use the sample dataset

Processes the 300 synthetic conversations in data/conversations.csv. With a paid API, try a limit of 10–20 first.
